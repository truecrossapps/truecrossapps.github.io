Privacy Policy

Screen Text Scanner · by True Cross Apps · Effective 4 November 2026

Screen Text Scanner ("the app") copies the text you see on your phone's screen so you can paste it anywhere. This policy explains what the app reads, what it keeps, and what third-party services it uses. In short: the text the app reads stays on your phone. We do not run any servers and we never receive your captured text.

What the app reads, and when

The app uses Android's Accessibility Service to read the text shown by the app you are looking at. It does this only when you tap or double-tap the floating bubble — never in the background on its own.

  • Tap: the app reads the on-screen text through Accessibility and scrolls the page to read the rest of it.
  • Double-tap (for pictures, PDFs and spreadsheets): the app takes screenshots while you scroll and reads the text in them with optical character recognition (OCR) that runs on your phone. Screenshots are processed in memory and discarded; they are never saved or uploaded.

The Accessibility Service is not used to collect any other information, and you can switch it off at any time in your phone's Settings › Accessibility.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • Captured text is copied to your clipboard and saved in the app's history (the last 5 captures on the free version, up to 500 with Pro). It is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Settings such as whether you have Pro, how many scans you have made today and whether you have seen the introduction.

You can delete individual captures or clear the whole history in the app (History › Clear history). Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Permissions

  • Accessibility Service — to read on-screen text and scroll when you tap the bubble, as described above.
  • Display over other apps — to show the floating bubble and the result pop-up over the app you are reading.
  • Foreground service and notification — to keep the bubble available while you use other apps.
  • Vibration — a short buzz if you scroll too fast while reading with OCR.
  • Internet — for ads and Google Play purchases only.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Captured text is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Bear in mind that anything copied to the clipboard can be pasted by you into other apps.
